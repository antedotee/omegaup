Source: `.audit/pr3/codemod-manual.txt`, "Manual conversion" section (9 entries). Paths are under `frontend/www/js/omegaup/`. Lines are the codemod report lines at commit 4e88dc6bd.

BS4 `.form-inline` is `display:flex; flex-flow:row wrap; align-items:center`. At `>=576px` it also sets `.form-group` and `label` to `margin-bottom:0` (labels become `flex`, centered), `.form-control`/`.custom-select` to `width:auto`, and `.form-check-input` to `margin:0 .25rem 0 0`. Each row below lists which of these the markup depended on and how BS5 utilities reproduce it. No omegaUp SCSS or test selector targeted `.form-inline`, `.input-group-prepend` or `.input-group-append` in these files, so no hook class was needed.
